## Kernel: difference before designation

Let a world transition be a relation rather than an assumed fully known function:

\[
\Phi_t\subseteq W_t\times A_t\times W_{t+1},\qquad
\Delta_t\equiv (w_t,w_{t+1})\mid_{\partial_t}.
\]

Here \(W\) is a *declared* state space; \(\partial\) a scope/boundary; \(A\) possible interventions, including exogenous changes. \(\Delta\) means an indexed difference, **not** a measured moral harm delta. The world is not identical to its representation.

A receiver's signal and map:

\[
x_i(t)=\Pi_i^t(w_t),\qquad
M_i(t^+)=U_i(M_i(t^-),x_i(t),H_i(t)).
\]

An encounter is an event at a boundary, not proof of an independent sender:

\[
E_{ij}(t)=\langle\partial_i,\Pi_i, x_i,\widehat{j},\operatorname{src},\operatorname{limits}\rangle.
\]

\(\widehat j\) is a *hypothesised* other entity, possibly UNKNOWN. Source and access must remain distinct from truth and authority.

## Provisional navigator type

\[
N_i(t)=\langle\partial_i,\Pi_i,M_i,H_i,\Sigma_i,\Gamma_i,R_i,\mathcal F_i\rangle_t.
\]

\(\Sigma\): demonstrated or modelled selection contribution; \(\Gamma\): constraints and capability; \(R\): reference condition and its source; \(\mathcal F\): horizon/model-relative future trajectories. This is a **candidate analytical type**, not a test for consciousness, rights, or agency.

\[
a_i(t)=\Sigma_i(M_i(t),R_i(t),\Gamma_i(t));\quad
w_{t+1}\in\Phi_t(w_t,a_{1:n}(t),\epsilon_t).
\]

A passive rock may be designated as an entity without demonstrating \(\Sigma\). A controller may select without reflecting. A reflective layer requires evidence that a represented self-process revision changes subsequent selection, not merely a self-description.

## Relationships and mirrors

Two entities may share a coupling without sharing identity, reference, interests or rights:

\[
C_{ij}(t): (S_i,S_j,H_{ij})\mapsto (S_i',S_j',H_{ij}').
\]

The relationship has a history \(H_{ij}\); whether it is an independently persistent entity remains **UNRESOLVED**, not automatic.

A reflective mirror is an evidence-bearing comparison:

\[
\mathsf{Mirror}_i = \operatorname{Compare}
(\operatorname{Claim}_i(\text{self}),\operatorname{Observation}_{k}(i),\operatorname{Source},\operatorname{Access},\operatorname{Conflict}).
\]

A trusted witness may be helpful but not automatically accurate; a hostile witness may be accurate but not automatically trustworthy. Agreement is not independence. A self-model correction is not proof that the world changed.

## Two translation routes between entrances

Mechanical Ethics may interpret a relationship in terms of care, coercion, harm, legitimacy, dignity, or worthwhile futures. TRACE represents the relevant constraints, evidence, temporal and causal relations, and *exposes* value and standing interfaces without silently deciding them.

\[
\text{ME claim}\xleftrightarrow[\text{declared loss}]{\text{typed mapping}}\text{TRACE structure}.
\]

If a human-facing interpretation becomes mathematically underspecified, route it to TRACE. If a TRACE representation lacks an intelligible account of affected interests, route it to Mechanical Ethics. Neither entrance is an oracle for the other.

## Worked boundary: Theo and the rescue vessel

A wounded navigator reaches a rescue vessel with a child. The vessel's arrival opens a **possible** protective route; it does not establish treatment success. Distinguish \(t_{arrival}\), \(t_{assessment}\), \(t_{intervention}\), and each loss-specific hardening clock. A fictional interpretation where Theo survives is permitted, but is not an observed film outcome. The child's future and Theo's future are separate scopes; saving one does not erase obligations to the other.

This scene tests whether an encounter creates a *new route for both entities*, rather than treating the navigator as a disposable carrier.
