# Build preliminary preservation and architecture review

16 November 2026. Status: PARTIAL; not a full v0.5 semantic audit and not a release recommendation. Source basis: released TRACE-v0.5.md at d688c149afc15c90ab51afaf08fe303771e9de20; candidate README.md and SYNTHESIS.md at 09c07a4b20134c6fccec2b6eb26fa4daf612bc5e. Campfire's final preservation manifest and simulator were not available in this snapshot. These are risks in applying the initial interface, not claims that the eventual manifest has omitted them.

## 1. Role-independent formation at risk

Source: v0.5 §2A, “The same encounter from B's side”, and “Negative compression fixture”. B's calibration work opens a possible independent teaching future even if B refuses another session. B is not merely a support input to A, and the absence of another session is not an obstacle to be overcome.

Counterexample: an E,V,C,F,N,P instance includes B as carrier, accurately records B's extra work, explicit refusal, and A's alternatives, but omits B's independent teaching possibility. All named records can remain populated and all A-goal comparisons unchanged, while the resulting account loses a positive formation pathway. The tuple alone does not guarantee retention; this is not a claim that the proposed V or P types prohibit recording it.

Repair: permit separate attributed or explicitly uncertain formation/prospect records for every affected participant, including supporters, independent of their instrumental role. Include a witness comparing two encodings identical on A-goal, burden and consent but differing in B's own possible future. The compact view should surface the difference or return UNKNOWN / expand when the use depends on B's future. Ask B rather than treating a third-party projection as B's endorsed goal.

## 2. Source assertion versus actual state and temporal custody

Source: §1 “Seven load-bearing distinctions”; §2A “Negative compression fixture”; §2 “Positive warrant and useful inherited profiles”. A report of deletion does not itself establish deletion; a corrected record does not establish effective protection or repair. A receipt can establish a defined status under a named evidence/authority contract without establishing the truth of historical contents.

Counterexample: a compact account records “data deleted; issue resolved” on receiving a report. In one world deletion occurred; in another, the report is mistaken and a copy remains. The same E field can encode either careful or collapsed representation, so typed membership alone supplies no guarantee.

Repair: preserve separate report/source/time, observation, status warranted by a named contract, confidence/applicability and currentness, actual effect unknown where not independently observed, downstream propagation and residual burden. Test both positive warrant (supported receipt under named contract) and negative non-upgrade (receipt does not prove deletion). A short view must link to the basis and show the qualifier when action depends on it. Do not require unsafe disclosure of witness information to establish provenance.

## 3. Renewal is not a timeless consent flag

Source: §1 and §2A, including “A's deadline is not B's obligation”; §2B conditional ordering across clocks. The prior calibration collaboration and prior agreement cannot imply a second session, a licence to reuse files, or availability at a later deadline.

Counterexample: two stories share A's desired target, B's earlier agreement, observed earlier assistance and current resource capacity. In only one B has agreed to a second session. A single boolean “consent = yes” for the encounter makes both appear equally executable.

Repair: represent actor, action, scope, time/expiry, revocation and renewal for each agreement, and separate agreement from the material ability to refuse. Preserve “not agreed/unknown” without inventing refusal. When deadline matters, order the exact agreement, action and intervention clocks. Test the paired stories with an otherwise identical spine.

## 4. Reference-only donor boundaries

Source: v0.5 §8 “Source coverage of the navigator addition” and §2 “Positive warrant and useful inherited profiles”. The release itself marks some v0.3 contracts as reference-only, including safe witness custody, workload/scheduling, recursion, enforcement/brake/rollback. The reference and its qualification are part of v0.5 meaning; the full donor contracts are not silently incorporated as fully reproduced or executed in v0.5.

Counterexample: a generated crosswalk states “v0.5 implements witness-safe disclosure and rollback”, based solely on cited donor labels. This simultaneously overstates the release and erases the useful route to the stronger donor.

Repair: for each unit use a disposition such as REPRODUCED / COMPRESSED / REFERENCE_ONLY / NEW_COMPOSITION, exact source anchors and limits, plus any explicit new 0.6 destination. A future candidate may choose to incorporate a donor in full, but must distinguish its new scope and verify it. Do not treat a link's presence as semantic proof.

## Proposed architecture and acceptance

One versioned source graph contains exact released source units, candidate destinations and typed relationships (preserves, qualifies, references, replaces with explicit rationale, unresolved). It distinguishes current source claims from proposed candidate semantics. Generate complete and spine views from the same versioned candidate, each with its use class, source/expansion anchors, omission conditions and an explicit UNKNOWN/expand path. Keep witness privacy separated from a public provenance pointer. Different use classes can require different included distinctions; neither a fixed word count nor an anchor count is proof of faithful use.

Test the three paired cases above against an independently read source, candidate complete view and generated spine. Predeclare decision and material distinctions, record exact outputs and loss, require a downstream decision rather than keyword recall, and include a constructive positive case as well as failures. Add a baseline ordinary prose compression and measure burden and false confidence. Semantic equivalence for every future use is not established by passing finite tests.
