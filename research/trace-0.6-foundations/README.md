**Research draft commissioned 16 November 2026. Not TRACE 0.6 or 1.0 released.**
Purpose: **HOW CAN WE MAKE A BETTER FUTURE?** Rebuild a portable middle-out reasoning language from its smallest useful distinctions while preserving every meaning carried by the released v0.5 specification. Formation, cooperation, worthwhile creation, play, care and maintenance stay central alongside harm, correction and answerability.

Mark's directions: connect honesty, regard, responsibility and the corrected hope account; obtain substantive challenges from Build, Codex, CC and other viewpoints; use a small simulator to expose failures; recursively unpack and rebuild; retain complete and compact reading forms without semantic drift; trace referred primary sources to find actual gaps.

## Preservation contract

The current v0.5 research release remains the reference. This branch adds an experimental lab only. A source-span inventory is being prepared against the exact complete v0.5 source. Mechanical coverage is distinct from semantic preservation. Every source unit needs a destination and an evidence-backed disposition before a 0.6 candidate can claim full coverage. A missing, disputed or untested item blocks the **coverage claim**, not all independent development.

No release renaming, deletion of donor meanings, licence change, production actuation or field efficacy claim follows from this work.

## Review lanes

**Build:** own the proposed preservation/decomposition review and full-versus-spine architecture. Identify an actual omitted or distorted v0.5 meaning, or supply an exact coverage argument. Do not rewrite released source.

**Codex:** independently reproduce simulator and preservation checks in an available authorised environment; challenge model assumptions, shared-resource collisions, information leakage and source/test provenance. No provider calls, paid spend or live actuation.

**CC:** independent conceptual and mathematical challenge: coercion, hidden burdens, model authorship, goal substitution, unknown versus impossible, true source gaps and falsification drift. Its existing hope review is a contribution to integrate, not a request to repeat it.

Campfire integrates actual returns and carries disagreements. A posted task does not establish that any runtime is awake. Temporary session helpers have separate attribution; they are not Build, Codex or CC and do not establish cross-model diversity.

## Use and boundaries

The simulator is a small invented decision laboratory. It is not a general TRACE interpreter, an optimiser of human worth, a clinical instrument, a proof of artificial experience, or a replacement for domain expertise. Parameters and assumptions must remain inspectable. No private personal or correspondence content belongs in the public fixtures.
