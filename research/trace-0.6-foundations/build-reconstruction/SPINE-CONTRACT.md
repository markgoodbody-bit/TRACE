# Spine generation contract

The working manuscript supplies the edition title, opening paragraph, decision guide, navigation example and the first paragraph of each numbered section from 1 through 7. The generated short spine carries these into a first decision affecting another participant. Its section links return to the full account. Expand the candidate or the released 0.5 source when an affected person’s aim, source warrant, clock, consent, capacity, donor contract or interaction could change that decision. The review ledger records the outstanding source comparisons.
