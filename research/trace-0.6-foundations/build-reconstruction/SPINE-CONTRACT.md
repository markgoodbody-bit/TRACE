# Spine generation contract

The generated spine starts with the candidate's decision guide in full and selects passages from its numbered sections. Its named use is a first decision about an intervention with a possible affected party. It includes source-section links and the UNRESOLVED inheritance warning. Expand the complete candidate and released v0.5 when the affected person's own aims, source warrant, controlled clock, consent renewal, material capacity, donor contract or complex interaction could change the decision. The selection is authored; no guarantee of semantic completeness follows from generation.
