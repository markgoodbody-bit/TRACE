# Reading the earlier TRACE editions

Start with the [0.6 manuscript](CANDIDATE.md). This guide identifies which earlier text to open if the next decision needs more than the example provides. All three source editions are included in the repository. Each keeps its own terminology, time basis and evidence conditions.

## Where the earlier maths and structure live

| --- | --- |

## The compatibility floor

The [fifteen clauses in 0.6](CANDIDATE.md#compatibility-floor-explicit-review-meanings) give a first reader a place to start. The [0.5 floor](../../../TRACE-v0.5.md#inherited-v04-compatibility-floor-review-level-semantics) names the clauses and the [0.4 compact specification](../../../TRACE-SPINE.md) supplies their original conditions. Use these four groups for a stronger claim.

| Clauses | What must survive the handover |
| --- | --- |
| F01–F04 | Keep the selection and its omissions, material dependencies, warrant at use, and the actual access and independence of each witness. [0.4 §5.1](../../../TRACE-SPINE.md#L91-L108) carries changed capabilities, relationships, constraints, scope, burdens and alternatives into the next reading. A check can have run and still have missed the decisive fault. |
| F05–F08 | A group outcome needs a supported correspondence before it can establish individual repair. Carry the holder and bounds of authority, actual routes for refusal, compatible clocks, feasible completion, and the bearer and kind of each residual cost. The [0.4 correction rule](../../../TRACE-SPINE.md#91-strong-window-claims) asks for a feasible positive route or evidence that the necessary route and its represented alternatives close. |
| F09–F12 | Name the baseline, horizon, measure and participants before comparing futures. A recurring failure needs evidence of mechanism. The reader can challenge the chosen observation boundary. A receiver may decline to disclose or act. See [0.4 §§12–16](../../../TRACE-SPINE.md#12-future-space). |
| F13–F15 | Attributed internal mechanisms need an empirical link. A reader can hand a decision to the person with authority before further inquiry spends the remaining time. A practical-advantage claim needs a named comparison, burden and outcome specified in advance. See [0.4 §§17–20](../../../TRACE-SPINE.md#17-mechanistic-interpretability-interface). |

## Technical expansion

The complete [0.3 technical reference](../../../TRACE.md), [0.4 compact specification](../../../TRACE-SPINE.md) and [0.5 research specification](../../../TRACE-v0.5.md) retain their published bytes. When a detailed profile is used, its fields, guards and worked limits travel together. The [case and donor index](DONOR-AND-CASE-INDEX.md) gives more routes into the sources. Particularly consequential ones include [trajectory correspondence](../../../TRACE.md#71-trajectory-correspondence-before-set-comparison), [carrying limits out of a recursive inspection](../../../TRACE.md#11-recursive-zoom--mandelbrot-rule), [an unresolved commitment receipt](../../../TRACE.md#143-unresolved-commitment-receipt), and [enforcement, braking and rollback](../../../TRACE.md#17-live-interpreter--value-layer--selector--connected-brake). The named person or system still needs the means and authority to act.

The [editorial ledger](review-decisions.json) records each decision on the 0.5 source spans. Where the full notation, historical record, or conditions of a fixture matter, the destination is the original passage. Where the new reading carries a distinction, the destination is the 0.6 passage. Entries about a historical example keep that example’s assumptions. The [earlier comparisons](SEMANTIC-REVIEW.md) preserve the gaps identified while the edition was being built. These are editorial decisions about what to read. They supply no assurance of field effectiveness or executable equivalence between editions.
