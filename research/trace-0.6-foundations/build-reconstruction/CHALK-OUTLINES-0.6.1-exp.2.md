## One song through several apertures

The opening is one person in bed, hoping morning brings a different self. The refrain brings in “we”. Both singers can make the experience recognisable, while the shared words carry the risk that each person's experience will be represented by the same outline. A chalk outline can tell you somebody was there. It can become all that a later observer sees of them. ## The form of hope

*Hi Ren* puts the conflict in one performed body. The ending revisits it through years of experience and describes learning to move with recurrence. It makes a further wager that music can reach someone after the maker has gone. In *Chalk Outlines*, hope initially asks for relief or transformation. The good day brings the possibility of keeping something that already exists. The duet makes a third possibility audible. Something a person has had to carry alone can be given a shared form, which a listener can recognise from a life neither singer knows. That possibility is compatible with the refrain returning and the trouble still being present. [*Hi Ren* performance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s_nc1IVoMxc) · [*Hi Ren* closing text](https://www.karafun.com/karaoke/ren/hi-ren/) · [*Chalk Outlines* lyrics](https://www.karafun.com/karaoke/ren/chalk-outlines/)

The clocks change with the aperture. There is the next breath, the night until morning, the good day and the time the song travels through other people's lives. A conclusion about one clock can leave the others open. The person remains the source for what it felt like and what is worth keeping. A listener's return can give evidence of a particular new use.

## From the song to a TRACE account

Use Π, x, B, σ and G as in the [released selective-causal loop](TRACE-v0.5-reference.md#1-orientation-difference-before-verdict). The state labels here name two possibilities in a fictional scene.

```text
x = Π(observer, t)(world with ease)   = smile
x = Π(observer, t)(world with effort) = smile
B(observer | x)                       = {ease, effort, possibly both}
G(person)                             = their stated aim, or ?
```

A selector with the same evidence makes the same choice or uses the same choice distribution in these cases. If the cases call for different offers, the navigator can ask a question that changes the choice or find an option that works in either. A practical question about keeping a quiet slot can preserve a route while the person keeps their reasons private. Acquiescence and replies both require their own accounts. A question can cost a choice if it takes too long.

**What the outline drops.** Words and symbols can carry the witness, the uncertain cause, the reason, the timing and the possible response. Timbre, pacing, anticipation and the effect of hearing a second person enter can change what a listener takes from the same words. Point back to the source at the point where that difference matters. A useful compression gives the next reader the means to notice and contest the missing part.

## Hearing another voice and being able to answer

Consider a fictional duet. The joint phrase needs an answer one beat after the cue, by beat four. A fixed part enters at beat three. A second performer can instead hear the cue and answer one beat later. The performers agree to the two core timings at beats two and three.

| Partner's cue | Fixed entrance | Responsive entrance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beat 2 | Beat 3, fits | Beat 3, fits |
| Beat 3 | Beat 3, misses | Beat 4, fits |
| Beat 4, timing variation | Beat 3, misses | Beat 5, outside the window |
| Partner declines | A solo remains a separate choice | The joint phrase is unavailable |

The first row gives the listener the same result with different dependencies. With equal weight on the first two stipulated worlds, the fixed entrance succeeds with probability 1/2 and the responsive entrance with probability 1. The +1/2 is about that joint phrase under those cue conditions. The hypothetical late cue can be heard and still fail to affect the phrase. Research on joint musical timing distinguishes anticipation and adjustment. A pianist study manipulated access to a responsive partner separately from how the interaction was perceived. These ideas help frame a possible comparison for the song without assigning it the result of the experiment. [Keller, Novembre and Hove, 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4240961/) · [Demos et al., 2017](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.00149/full)

## The navigator's return

Apply both standing-team results. [Codex](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/issues/954#issuecomment-7112970742) puts the attempt at minute 30 and the end of the check at minute 50, after departure at minute 40. [CC](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/issues/954#issuecomment-7113175471) asks what follows the learner home even if the earlier check does finish. The useful next offer has to carry the reason through both changes of scope.

```text
G(at workshop) = unaided attempt + qualified check completed by departure
G(at home)     = ability to repeat the work + a usable way to get a safety judgement
Observed       = the attempt in this session
Check by departure = false in the late-check branch
Home outcome   = ? under either earlier offer
```

Competent support and the learner's own ability may both matter at home. A later slot, a willing qualified helper or another access route can be proposed with its own conditions. Preserve what the learner actually did and what they still want to try. The helper can decline. Another qualified person may have a way to support the next decision.

[The quiet room](navigator-0.6.1-exp.2.html) uses opening choices, distinct accounts and a return. It also carries a small, versioned JSON record in the same file. A text reader can inspect the whole source. A browser reader can choose which account to open first and see how their first choice fares after another person speaks. The original performance is linked in this note so a reader can return to the voice and timing rather than inherit our interpretation as the whole song. These are three ways to carry meaning. Whether a different reader changes a consequential choice after the encounter is open.
