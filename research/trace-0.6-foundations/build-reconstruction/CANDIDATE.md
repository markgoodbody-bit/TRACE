# TRACE 0.6 reconstruction — research candidate

Status: INCOMPLETE. This is an intelligible proposed architecture, not a replacement release. All v0.5 units have exact source spans in crosswalk.json; their semantic dispositions remain unresolved until compared to this reconstruction. Read the source where material. Existing v0.5 reference-only donor contracts remain references, not newly claimed implementations.

## 1. Begin with a living situation

An entity, group or institution encounters a changing world through bounded observation. The world, signals, accounts, maps and retained records are different. A boundary and a participant list are provisional, not declarations of moral standing. Other entities have perspectives and possibilities of their own. Ask what is happening and what could be formed, enjoyed, maintained, repaired or lost, before choosing a verdict. Honest uncertainty includes unknown, unsupplied, false, disputed and out-of-scope; lack of a recorded route is not proof that no route exists.

For each consequential claim name its source, scope, time, evidence and relevant competing account. Separate a report from the event reported, the status warranted under a named contract from the truth of its whole contents, and a corrected record from a changed world or repaired harm. Witnesses may need protected custody; a useful record must not force unsafe disclosure. Send, receipt, understanding and effective downstream use differ.

## 2. People, relationships and possibilities

Ask whose lives, aims and burdens could change; whose voice is absent; who may contest the framing; and what the account does not yet know. A stated goal has an author, context and history. Record evidence about the options and constraints under which it formed without overriding the person's own aim. An aim revision does not retroactively make an earlier failure a success.

Supporters are not only resources for a beneficiary: they can form capabilities and futures of their own, including through cooperation, care, learning and maintenance. A possible route in a map, material access to it, chosen participation and sustainable support are different. One participant's benefit does not establish another's consent or obligation.

## 3. Offers, duties and power

Distinguish offers, prior agreement, ongoing commitments, externally grounded duties and performed actions. Preserve each holder, ground, scope, recipient, trigger, due time, support dependency, renewal, refusal, withdrawal and usable exit where material. Acceptance under pressure is not automatically free agreement. A capacity shortage does not itself erase a duty or settle blame. A choice between competing acceptable plans needs an accountable holder, affected interests and contest route; an equation does not supply legitimacy.

## 4. Change, interaction and evidence of influence

Where a bounded engineered dependency is warranted, name component relations and conditional tests; keep proposed connections distinct from confirmed ones. Do not make association or an editable graph a proof of causal attribution. Where relationships co-evolve and the model cannot support a decomposed causal path, describe interacting constraints and dispositions, diverse plausible explanations, observation and small revisable interventions; do not force additive contribution weights or invented probabilities. Both modes support action, not a claim that the situation has been classified correctly forever.

For each intervention, name who may act, what may change, affected scopes, reversibility, indicators, observation limits and a return or stop route. Distinguish feasibility, permission, choice, actuation and actual effect. Unknown evidence can be controlled: identify who authors, controls or can pause the clock, who controls observation timing, who benefits from delay and who bears its costs. UNKNOWN is not automatically neutral or bad faith.

## 5. Clocks, correction and construction

Track the relevant event ordering. A review after a route closes may improve the record without restoring that route. Protective effect before hardening is strict where specified; a separate delivery-by-deadline contract may be inclusive. A correction route requires accessible capacity and time, not merely a published address. Preserve unattempted alternatives and residual burden when shared capacity cannot satisfy all obligations.

Compare declared alternatives using the same stated affected scope, aim, protective measures, burden units, baseline, horizon and assumptions; disclose frame changes. A model-relative forecast is not a guaranteed world outcome. A promise is not delivery; delivering one component of a joint effort can cost a contributor even if the other component never arrives. Rest, companionship or maintenance need not show an increase in a selected score to matter.

## 6. A compact view with an escape hatch

One versioned source graph can serve different readers. A complete account carries the situated distinctions above plus the source units and unresolved qualifications in the crosswalk. A spine selects a named use: perturb a value, source, actor, scope or time; if that can change the action, burden, authority or interpretation, retain it or mark the condition for expansion. Source spans and checksums prove neither semantic retention nor reader understanding. An omitted material fact requires UNKNOWN or expansion, not an all-clear. Stopping a bounded inquiry is not failure if the unresolved question, receiving owner and usable handoff survive.

## 7. Paired worked probe

**Engineered dependency.** A technician proposes a backup restore. A person has offered assistance, but no one has confirmed availability for this specific run. The old approval covers a different backup. Next: retain the old decision as history, verify the specific restoration target and renewed authority, reserve a fallback and record when its usefulness expires. A graph can expose known prerequisites, not prove that every dependency has been discovered.

**Co-evolving support.** Two neighbours consider a shared transport arrangement; one is learning to teach a skill as part of it. Changes in trust, cost and other people's availability may change the arrangement. Next: ask each participant what they want and can refuse, agree a small optional trial and an observation/review time, and leave alternatives available. Avoid assigning additive causes or probabilities without evidence. If a neighbour declines, their independent teaching possibility remains in the account.

These are invented examples. They show candidate use, not measured superiority, real-world effect, consent or actual authority.

## 8. Research and unresolved inheritance

Released 0.5 remains authoritative for its edition. The mechanical crosswalk and reconstructed sections make a review possible; they do not establish that every fine-grained source meaning has been preserved. The donor profiles, source crosswalk, cases, glossary, evidence limits and within-case conditional bindings all require unit-by-unit semantic inspection. This draft is not an executable TRACE interpreter, legal conclusion, moral decision-maker or general theory of complex systems. Primary literature and a cold independent full/spine/prose comparison remain open.
