The booking record

One learner attended and made an unaided attempt. The organiser can count a completed booking. The choice of next session still depends on what the learner came to be able to do.

The learner's answer

“I could hear myself think in there. I got a turn with the tools. Next week I'll be on my own with my bike. I'd like to know what I can do then, and who to ask if I'm unsure.”

The facilitator's answer

The learner finished the attempt at minute 30. The facilitator can start the check at minute 40 and it takes ten minutes. At minute 40 the learner has to leave. The facilitator offered help today and has yet to decide about next Saturday.

Go back to your first answer

Carry forward the learner's unaided attempt, the support the quiet room supplied, and the check that could not be completed in time. Ask what would make a further quiet slot usable and who could give the learner a way to get a qualified judgement next week. The learner can consider that offer when it exists.

If the check had finished today, the person heading home would still need a route they can use there. The offer to return and the capability at home have their own evidence.
