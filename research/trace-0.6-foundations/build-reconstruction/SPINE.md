Research candidate. The [review ledger](review-decisions.json) records source comparisons. Unreviewed material remains UNRESOLVED.

Use class: a first decision on an intervention affecting another participant. Expand the candidate and the released source when omitted detail changes authority, burden, consent, evidence, time or a constructive possibility. A missing material distinction means UNKNOWN, not permission.

## Start with the next decision

1. **Give the account a job.** Name what someone needs to understand, choose or make possible now, whose purposes might differ, and the period that matters. Let the inquiry change the question. If you are using Mechanical Ethics, state which commitments guide this use, such as making harm visible, keeping correction reachable or building useful capability without hiding its costs.
2. **Follow what could change it.** Keep the source, status and scope of evidence the next step depends on. When something seems to have failed, ask what observation could distinguish the plausible causes before assigning a repair. For a choice on a clock, work back from the effect to the latest useful answer. Carry unresolved questions that could change the choice.
3. **Find a usable next move.** Check who can take it, with what agreement, authority, time and resources. Help keep a willing alternative within reach while a material question is being resolved. For protection, follow the move through to its effect before the particular loss hardens.
4. **Return what someone can use.** Carry what actually happened, any new ability or changed purpose, unresolved costs and what would require a fresh decision. End with a next act or handoff someone can take up.

## 1. Begin with a living situation

An entity, group or institution encounters a changing world through bounded observation. The world, signals, accounts, maps and retained records are different. A boundary and a participant list are provisional, not declarations of moral standing. Other entities have perspectives and possibilities of their own. Ask what is happening and what could be formed, enjoyed, maintained, repaired or lost, before choosing a verdict. Keep what a source asserts separate from its warrant, our access to it and anyone's decision. An assertion can have a definite value while its evidence is unavailable or disputed. A missing answer is different again; silence alone establishes neither withholding nor refusal. Lack of a recorded route is not proof that no route exists.

## 2. People, relationships and possibilities

Ask whose lives, aims and burdens could change; whose voice is absent; who may contest the framing; and what the account does not yet know. A stated goal has an author, context and history. Record evidence about the options and constraints under which it formed without overriding the person's own aim. Assess a result against the aim and criterion in the commitment it concerns. When someone states a new aim, preserve the earlier commitment and date the new choice. Keep any inference about their aim attributed to the observer. Ask which useful routes each participant can take on their own and which depend on others. Read a recorded choice alongside the options the person could use at the time. [See how that changes the next offer](CANDIDATE.md#a-choice-after-the-options-change).

## 3. Offers, duties and power

Distinguish offers, prior agreement, ongoing commitments, externally grounded duties and performed actions. Preserve each holder, ground, scope, recipient, trigger, due time, support dependency, renewal, refusal, withdrawal and usable exit where material. Before inviting reliance on a firm offer, check that essential support is agreed and can be delivered in time. Record who undertakes the commitment, their authority where it matters, and what the offer reasonably supports. If support changes, identify who can reach the people relying on it while they still have a usable choice and who can address losses caused by that reliance. Acceptance under pressure is not automatically free agreement. A capacity shortage does not itself erase a duty or settle blame. A choice between competing acceptable plans needs an accountable holder, affected interests and contest route; an equation does not supply legitimacy.

## 4. Change, interaction and evidence of influence

Where a bounded engineered dependency is warranted, name component relations and conditional tests; keep proposed connections distinct from confirmed ones. Do not make association or an editable graph a proof of causal attribution. Where relationships co-evolve and the model cannot support a decomposed causal path, describe interacting constraints and dispositions, diverse plausible explanations, observation and small revisable interventions; do not force additive contribution weights or invented probabilities. Both modes support action, not a claim that the situation has been classified correctly forever.

## 5. Clocks, correction and construction

Track the relevant event ordering. A review after a route closes may improve the record without restoring that route. Protective effect before hardening is strict where specified; a separate delivery-by-deadline contract may be inclusive. A correction route requires accessible capacity and time, not merely a published address. Preserve unattempted alternatives and residual burden when shared capacity cannot satisfy all obligations. For a scheduled opportunity, check what enables each participant to use it in the time available. For a proposed pause, trace the warning to someone who can act within scope, then ask how they can verify the effect before the boundary. The [dispatch case](CANDIDATE.md#a-warning-before-dispatch) works through this route.

## 6. A compact view with an escape hatch

One versioned source graph can serve different readers. Before shortening an account, name the conclusion and its intended use. Change a premise's value, source, actor, scope or time and ask whether the supported conclusion, permitted action or distribution of burdens changes. Keep the distinction if it does. Where the spine omits the detail, read it before relying on a conclusion it could change. Keep uncertainty about omissions visible. Record the changes considered so a reader can challenge anything overlooked. Keep each claim at the strength of its evidence, each aim with the person who holds it, and any limit the next decision depends on. The check applies to that decision. Claims about reader behaviour or completeness across uses need their own evidence. A bounded inquiry can end with the unresolved question and a usable route to someone who can take it up. ## 7. Paired worked probe

**One community learning project, two lenses.** A wants to learn to repair a bicycle. B offers to facilitate one evening and wants practice explaining repairs. C wants to take part but needs a quieter setting. A local group proposes an 18:00 session with a room and tools, and a driver expresses interest in shared transport. B's offer is for this one session; neither transport nor the room's access arrangement is yet confirmed. Learning, enjoyment, repair and possible future mutual help all matter. None creates permission to reuse someone's story or assume another session. At the return, check what would make one further session usable and who could keep it going.
