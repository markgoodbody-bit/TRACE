## 2. What survives

The field lesson survives in bounded form:

```text
FRESH_CONTEXT != COLD_RECEIVER
CURRENT_SEPARATION != HISTORICAL_INDEPENDENCE
CONTEXT_RESET != PROVENANCE_RESET
BLINDED_RATER != INDEPENDENT_RUBRIC
INDEPENDENT_EXECUTION != VALID_ESTIMATOR
```

These are not proposed as new invariants requiring carrier expansion here. They are useful worked distinctions recoverable through existing TRACE machinery when load-bearing.

The important operational rule remains:

> When independence matters to a claim, expose the provenance and aperture facts that make the claimed independence relevant; do not let a fresh session, different provider, external label, blinded identity, or independent execution silently certify stronger independence than the evidence supports.

## 3. Why ordinary TRACE is enough on the cases tested

Current TRACE v0.3 already carries the required structural machinery.

### Provenance / warrant

A load-bearing claim exposes source/provenance, observation or derivation route, aperture/access boundary, freshness, evidence pointer, contrary evidence, confidence and unknowns.

### Target-set aperture

Selection of what a review/comparison reaches can expose source, selection basis, targets, omissions, alternative target sets, comparison basis and custody/control.

### Verification discrimination

```text
CHECK_EXISTS != CHECK_EXECUTED
CHECK_EXECUTED != CHECK_DETECTS_TARGET_FAILURE
STATIC_CORRECTNESS != OPERATIONAL_DISCRIMINATION
```

### Witness ceiling

```text
EXTERNAL != INDEPENDENT
SEPARATE_PARTY != INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE
A_WITNESS_ESTABLISHES_ONLY_WHAT_ITS_APERTURE_SUPPORTS
```

Together these can represent the tested independence distinctions without a separate named `independence_exposure_view`.
