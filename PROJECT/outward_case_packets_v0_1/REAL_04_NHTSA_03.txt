FACTUAL MATERIAL FROM THE FROZEN REPORT

The reporting entity identified the subject vehicle as a 2021 Jaguar I-Pace operated by Waymo LLC and equipped with Waymo’s “5th Generation ADS”. The report states that the automated driving system was engaged and that the vehicle was within its operational design domain. Driver/operator type was reported as “None”.

The incident occurred in Phoenix, Arizona, in November 2022 at 08:48. The exact day and street address are redacted in the public data. Conditions were reported as daylight, clear weather, dry roadway, a street with no unusual roadway condition, and a posted speed limit of 25 mph.

The reporting entity’s narrative says that the Waymo vehicle was travelling eastbound when it encountered a stopped garbage truck travelling in the opposite direction. The Waymo vehicle moved to the right side of the street and stopped because it could not proceed past the garbage truck and an SUV parked parallel to the curb.

According to the narrative, the garbage truck then attempted to pass between the stopped Waymo vehicle and a parked passenger vehicle on the truck’s right. The garbage truck made contact with the left rear corner of the Waymo vehicle. The report states that at impact the Waymo vehicle’s Level 4 automated driving system was engaged in autonomous mode and the Waymo vehicle was stationary.

Structured fields report the other object as a heavy truck. The subject vehicle’s pre-crash movement was “Stopped” and its pre-crash speed was 0 mph. Property damage was reported. No injuries were reported. No air bags were reported deployed and the subject vehicle was not reported towed.

The report indicates that telematics, video and other data were available to the reporting entity. It also states that law enforcement was investigating and that the reporting entity or manufacturer was investigating. The frozen public packet does not contain those underlying telemetry, video, police-investigation or internal-investigation materials.

The narrative closes by stating that Waymo was reporting the crash under Request No. 2 of Standing General Order 2021-01 and might supplement or correct its reporting if additional information became available. In the frozen archive carrier used for this study, this Report ID has only Version 1.
