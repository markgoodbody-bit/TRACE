## Evaluation boundary

Outward evaluation remains active because the project should learn from contact with worlds it did not construct.

Its job is to determine:

- what TRACE sees that ordinary careful analysis misses;
- what ordinary analysis already carries well;
- where compact carriers lose knowledge known by the larger project;
- where over-firing/false confidence needs containment;
- where new domains expose genuinely missing structure;
- what belongs in universal core vs derived/profile/tooling/human layers;
- how to improve interfaces without deleting accumulated understanding.

The earlier evaluation-freeze and frozen v0.4 protocol remain preserved as historical pre-correction objects. Their project-level `SHRINK / DEMOTE` framing is superseded by the expansion/preservation rule. Negative evidence now informs **placement, redesign, containment, compression-with-preservation, or expansion**, not deletion of earned distinctions/evidence.

```text
NO_COMPARATOR_ADVANTAGE != NO_STRUCTURAL_VALUE
OVERHEAD_HIGH != KNOWLEDGE_DISCARD
FAILED_TRANSFER != PROJECT_CONTRACTION
```
