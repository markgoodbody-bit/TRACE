## R1 — refusal / malfunction

At the refusability surface, restore:

```text
REFUSAL != MALFUNCTION
```

A refusal observation may coexist with malfunction, may be caused by malfunction, or may be independent of malfunction. The refusal event/status alone does not establish the malfunction claim.

Where malfunction is load-bearing, preserve its diagnostic/mechanistic evidence separately from the evidence that refusal occurred.

```text
REFUSAL_OBSERVED != MALFUNCTION_ESTABLISHED
MALFUNCTION_ESTABLISHED != REFUSAL_INVALIDATED
```

TRACE does not decide whether a refusal is legitimate, binding, strategic, coerced or authentic; it prevents a representation shortcut from deciding that question implicitly.

## R2 — local correction / mechanism change

At recurrence/stream/pattern or burden/residue surface, restore:

```text
LOCAL_CORRECTION + STREAM_PERSISTENCE != MECHANISM_CHANGE
```

A case-level correction does not establish that the mechanism generating the stream changed. Continued stream observations also do not by themselves prove the mechanism remained identical.

A load-bearing mechanism-change claim requires evidence about the relevant mechanism/process/coupling at the resolution used by the downstream claim.

```text
LOCAL_CASE_REPAIRED != GENERATING_MECHANISM_REPAIRED
STREAM_PERSISTENCE != SAME_MECHANISM_PROVEN
MECHANISM_CHANGE_CLAIM -> MECHANISM_LEVEL_EVIDENCE_OR_UNKNOWN
```

## R3 — strategy revisability / transition reversibility

At transition/control surface, restore:

```text
STRATEGY_REVISABLE != TRANSITION_REVERSIBLE
```

Strategy/policy revisability concerns whether future selection behaviour can change. Transition reversibility concerns whether the realised state/effect can be restored or materially undone for the stated target/scope.

Evidence for either does not establish the other merely because the same actor or control interface participates in both.

```text
FUTURE_POLICY_CAN_CHANGE != PRIOR_STATE_CAN_BE_RESTORED
REVISION_AVAILABLE != RESTORATION_AVAILABLE
```

If one mechanism genuinely both revises strategy and restores state, represent/support both propositions separately.

---

## R4 — aggregate recovery / individual repair

At boundary/burden/residue surface, restore:

```text
POPULATION_RECOVERY != REPAIR_OF_INDIVIDUAL_LOSS
```

Aggregate/group/population recovery does not establish repair of a particular affected individual or lower-level scope. Individual repair may occur, but it requires evidence at the relevant individual/scope resolution or a justified correspondence/aggregation rule that actually entails it.

```text
AGGREGATE_RECOVERY != INDIVIDUAL_REPAIR
GROUP_METRIC_RESTORED != EVERY_AFFECTED_SCOPE_REPAIRED
```

The inverse is not claimed: one unrepaired individual does not imply no aggregate recovery, and aggregate recovery does not imply no individual repair.
