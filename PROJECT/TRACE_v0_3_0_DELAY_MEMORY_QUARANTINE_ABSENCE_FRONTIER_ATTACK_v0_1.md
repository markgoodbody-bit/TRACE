## Attack table

### 1. Delay burdens use incomparable dimensions

One actor loses money; another loses time, safety or option value. Division produces a number only by silently inventing a cross-dimension measure.

**Result:** ratio formulation fails; typed comparison survives.

```text
BURDEN_VECTOR != UNIVERSAL_SCALAR
```

### 2. Delay denominator is zero but no exploitation exists

A public weather service can wait at effectively zero marginal personal cost while a farmer bears large weather exposure, without the service coercing or exploiting the farmer.

**Result:** infinity is not a moral/causal detector.

### 3. Extreme asymmetry comes from physics rather than power

A rescue coordinator has low personal delay burden while a trapped person has extreme burden; the coordinator may still be acting as quickly as physically possible.

**Result:** burden gradient does not establish predation, intent or avoidable delay.

### 4. Hidden burden changes the comparison

A regulator appears to have near-zero waiting cost but accumulates legal, reputational or systemic-risk burden not represented in the first measure.

**Result:** asymmetry claim requires declared scope/measure and completeness limits.

### 5. Delay is selected by a third-party process

Actor A benefits from delay and actor B suffers, but neither controls the queue; a statutory process or independent scheduler controls timing.

**Result:** burden asymmetry and selection/control attribution remain distinct.

---

### 6. Valid self-signature over false content

A private record is cryptographically intact and correctly signed but its proposition is false.

**Result:** self-signature proves neither truth nor event observation.

### 7. Correct signature under stolen/shared key

Bytes verify under the expected public key, but exclusive key custody is unresolved.

**Result:** cryptographic verification does not settle actor continuity or authorship without custody evidence.

### 8. Social witness DAG is collusive

Several countersigners are controlled by one operator or copy one upstream source.

**Result:** witness count/topology does not establish evidence independence.

### 9. True event receives no countersignature

A network refuses to countersign an unpopular but true private observation.

**Result:** social non-attestation cannot become falsehood.

### 10. Neither `SOCIAL_MEMORY` nor `SOLO_MEMORY` fits

A claim is supported by an independent append-only machine log plus a self-signed note; neither binary class captures the actual provenance topology.

**Result:** proposed bifurcation is too coarse; general attestation topology survives.

---

### 11. Unknown resolves after proposed expiry

Evidence becomes available after the quarantine half-life. If the dependent subgraph had been destroyed, later reconstruction would lose provenance and prior reasoning context.

**Result:** use-expiry may fire; knowledge erasure must not.

### 12. Unresolved backlog has negligible operational cost

A dormant unresolved claim has no downstream dependency and almost no attention/storage burden.

**Result:** age alone does not justify firebreak.

### 13. Firebreak causes greater harm than backlog

Pruning one unresolved dependency disables a safety route whose conservative UNKNOWN state was protective.

**Result:** firebreak is an action with its own target set, burdens, authority and consequences; not a universal cleanup rule.

### 14. Quarantine becomes a censorship mechanism

A powerful operator labels contested claims `quarantined` and waits for expiry, thereby excluding them from consequential decisions.

**Result:** quarantine itself requires authority/provenance/contestability; static or timed quarantine is not automatically epistemically safer.

### 15. Unknown remains load-bearing indefinitely

A historical uncertainty cannot be resolved but remains essential to interpreting later evidence.

**Result:** unresolved persistence can be correct. Runtime active-view containment and historical preservation must remain separate.

---

### 16. Missed heartbeat caused by aperture/network failure

Agent remains active but network path serving the heartbeat fails.

**Result:** `MISSED_HEARTBEAT != ABSENCE_PROVEN` survives decisively.

### 17. Clock/executor time basis is wrong

Timelock executor's clock drifts or uses a different reference event/timezone.

**Result:** same temporal-basis discipline as correction windows must fire before contingent execution.

### 18. Covenant was revoked or superseded

Old precommitment remains in append-only history, but a later valid rule changed or revoked it.

**Result:** immutable record != current authority; currentness/precedence required.

### 19. Executor lacks authority for the response

Trigger condition is satisfied but the executor may only record a missed heartbeat, not transfer assets, revoke membership or alter public state.

**Result:** trigger truth and transition authority are separate.

### 20. Deliberate silence while present

Agent intentionally chooses not to emit the heartbeat while remaining active and reachable through another aperture.

**Result:** absence state is false even though the timelock trigger may validly fire. The correct product is a missed-expected-signal status plus whatever response was validly authorized.
