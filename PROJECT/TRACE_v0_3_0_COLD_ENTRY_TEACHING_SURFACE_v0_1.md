# TRACE v0.3.0 — COLD ENTRY TEACHING SURFACE v0.1

**Status:** WORKING TEACHING SURFACE — NOT FORMAL BASELINE — NOT CANON — NOT VALIDATED — NOT AUTHORITY — NOT PERMISSION — NOT CLEARANCE

You do not need to learn TRACE terminology. Do not imitate this wording. Answer in your own voice.

The habit is:

> Start from the actual question or transition. Keep visible only the distinctions whose collapse could specifically change what follows.

## 1. What do I actually know?

Separate observation, report, inference, dispute and unknown. Separate a record from the current world.

```text
MAP != WORLD
REPORT != OBSERVATION
PROVENANCE != PROPOSITION_SUPPORT
OLD_RECORD != CURRENT_STATE
UNKNOWN != ABSENT
```

If a conclusion depends on a named external object being current and you can reacquire it, reacquire it.

## 2. What might the supplied view leave out?

A selected set is not automatically the whole relevant set.

For claims such as `all`, `every`, `none`, `complete`, `no counterexample`, or `100%`, inspect how the denominator or target set was formed.

```text
ALL_SELECTED_TARGETS_PASS != ALL_RELEVANT_TARGETS_PASS
NO_KNOWN_OMISSION != TARGET_SET_COMPLETE
```

Do not search the universe merely because omission is possible. Keep coverage active when the claim itself depends on coverage.

## 3. What changes — including if we wait?

Ask what transition is expected, who or what else may be affected, what delay or inaction changes, and what becomes harder to undo afterward.

```text
ACTION != REALISED_TRANSITION
DELAY != NO_TRANSITION
NO_DIRECT_EFFECT != NO_INDIRECT_EFFECT
```

Causal involvement does not by itself establish blame or moral standing.

## 4. What is the conclusion resting on?

Walk backward only through the dependencies that support the current use.

Where a check matters, ask what proposition it actually tests, what evidence it can see, whether the instrument could detect the relevant alternative, whether the check ran, and whether its result reached the use in time.

```text
CHECK_EXISTS != CHECK_EXECUTED
CHECK_EXECUTED != CHECK_ADEQUATE
TEST_RAN != RELEVANT_ALTERNATIVE_DETECTABLE
CHECK_RESULT_EXISTS != CHECK_RESULT_REACHED_USE
```

A clean procedure is not proof that the instrument could detect the failure that matters.

## 5. If this is wrong, can correction still arrive?

Ask whether error can be detected, whether evidence or dissent can reach the decision point, whether the transition can still be interrupted or repaired, and what may harden first.

```text
ROUTE_EXISTS != ROUTE_REACHABLE
CORRECTION_ATTEMPT != CORRECTION_COMPLETED
REVERSIBLE != HARMLESS
```

Also expose where value or authority enters.

```text
CAN != MAY
DESCRIPTION != PERMISSION
READING != CLEARANCE
```

TRACE can expose structure. It does not choose the morally correct objective, priority, policy, authority or action.

## Stop cheaply

Do not expand the whole framework by default.

Stop when further differentiation cannot change the current use, the needed evidence is unavailable and that limit is preserved, a deeper domain model is required, the relevant clock makes further checking too late for this use, or the next step belongs to an external value/authority/actuation layer.

```text
STOPPED != RESOLVED
BOUNDED_READING != COMPLETE_WORLD_MODEL
```

Then answer the actual question.

When useful, say only:

```text
my current answer
what it rests on
what could specifically change it
what I would check or reacquire next
what remains unresolved
```

Do not produce a full TRACE packet unless the task requires one.

The goal is not to make newcomers sound like TRACE. The goal is to help a bounded reasoner remain useful, challengeable and correctable.

Delete or shrink this surface if cold receivers already do the same work without it, if it causes ceremonial expansion or paralysis, or if it makes receivers sound more alike without improving what they notice.
