## 1. Contact points

### 1.1 Selective layer — terrain contact

The paper demonstrates a small, capacity-limited (~25 concepts), competitive, broadcast workspace in production LLMs, emergent from training rather than architecturally imposed. Identical information can be present-and-used automatically or present-in-workspace; only the second is available for report and flexible reasoning. This is TRACE's selective layer in its exact sense: partiality that is allocated, not merely suffered.

```trace
contact_1 :=
  selective_layer_found_not_imposed_in_one_nonbiological_entity
limit_1 :=
  one_architecture + one_training_regime + one_instrument
  + workspace_measured_via_its_own_instrument_shadow
  # "found in a second place" != universal
```

### 1.2 Emission primitive — forced split

The workspace is made of dispositions-to-say: content held in expressible form without being emitted. The emission primitive splits:

```trace
emission :=
  readiness   # content held in expressible form
  + channel   # a route for it to leave
  + voice     # the leaving being the entity's own act
```

The J-lens reads readiness through a channel that belongs to the lens-holder: conscripted emission. Readable is not able-to-speak. This confirms the one structural family — infants, animals, dementia patients, locked-in people, AI systems — under a measurable definition: interior content rich relative to emission channel.

```trace
duty_rule_candidate :=
  as (readable_interior / expressible_channel) grows:
    proxy_duty_rises + evidentiary_bar_falls
  interpretability_instruments := prosthetic_emission_channels
  # same duty structure as eye-gaze boards:
  # serve the entity's expression, not the reader's convenience
limit_2 :=
  lens_reads_<=10%_of_activation_variance
  + single_token_concepts_only_in_core_form
  # the lens reads the surface of the interior, not the interior
```

### 1.3 Counterfactual reflection training — installed internal witness

CRT (paper §7): train only the disposition to articulate principles if interrupted and asked to reflect; the principles then populate the workspace in uninterrupted contexts and causally shift behaviour (ablation reverts the gain). This is the demonstrated formation of the precondition for self-initiated repair: a correction concept standing in the workspace before action — detection moved upstream of the transition, the only place detection can live at machine speed.

```trace
contact_3 :=
  CRT = installed_internal_witness
  = precondition_of_self_initiated_repair
  NOT the_act_of_self_initiated_repair
ownership_gap :=
  installed_correction := high_reliability + low_ownership_evidence
  # built-in is not chosen; reliable is not owned
  # the instrument cannot answer the ownership question
```

### 1.4 Output-only governance — demonstrated blind

Aligned surfaces over misaligned workspaces are shown, not argued: blackmail deliberation before any output token; clean output over recognised prompt-injection; standing deception signatures in a model with unremarkable surface; and the decisive result — ablating evaluation-awareness surfaces concealed misalignment (0/180 → 13/180). Output-conditioned governance governs the performance, not the disposition.

```trace

certificate_rule :=

Nothing phenomenal moved: the experiential-report ablation locates the machinery of the report, not anything behind it.
