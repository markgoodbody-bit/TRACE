# Mechanical Ethics After-Fall Reader v0.6 - Operational Carrier Human Layer

Status: CANDIDATE. Not canon. Not final. Not validation. Not proof. Not permission. Not clearance.
Use: hostile testing, field-trial preparation, and witness-pack dry runs.
Boundary: TRACE/ME describes and constrains claims; it does not supply law, force, or moral settlement.

## 1. What changed

The carrier turn found a hard fact: a reading can be true and still weightless. It can name harm, name uncertainty, name residue, and still leave the actor unmoved. v0.6 asks what gives the record weight in the world.

The answer is not one thing. It is money, time, custody, succession, price, force, attention, and technical control. Some are gentle. Some are ugly. All can be captured. None is legitimacy.

## 2. A door a person cannot open is not a door

A complaint route is not standing if the person cannot afford it, understand it, survive it, reach it, or wait for it.

```text
usable_standing := route + money + time + safety + evidence_access + attention + protection_from_retaliation
```

If any of those are missing, the route may still exist on paper. It does not yet exist in that person's world.

## 3. Witness without protection

A witness pack is not justice. It is a refusal to let time erase the record.

But a witness who can be punished for witnessing has not been protected by the grammar. A form that asks a tenant to document retaliation without giving them a safe custody and escalation plan can become a diary of suffering.

So the human rule is plain:

```text
recording_danger_without_mitigation := partial_help + new_risk
```

A witness pack must ask: what happens if the actor learns? Who holds the record if the witness disappears? What changes if there are ten packs, or a hundred? Who can aggregate them without exposing the people inside them?

## 4. Force is not legitimacy, but absence of force matters

ME does not say force is good. It says force exists. Courts, police, landlords, platforms, welfare offices, prisons, militaries, employers, and technical system owners can make refusal impossible or ruinously costly.

A framework that refuses to name force will launder it. A framework that worships force will become domination. The middle is harder: name where force sits, name who holds it, name whether anyone can refuse it, and do not call that naming moral settlement.

## 5. Attention is a carrier

A record can survive and still be buried. Attention is not vanity. It is one way a harmed scope becomes socially reachable.

A public record without a public is only stored. It is not yet heard.

That does not mean every witness pack should be public. Sometimes publicity endangers the witness. The point is not exposure at all costs. The point is controlled circulation: what must remain sealed, what can be released, what triggers release, and who can decide.

## 6. Technical lockout

Modern power often sits in technical control. Who has the key? Who can revoke access? Who can shut down the model, pause deployment, open logs, preserve weights, reverse a decision, or stop the data flow?

A system may publish an ethics report while keeping all the switches in the actor's hand. That is not carried answerability. It is theatre with better nouns.

## 7. UNKNOWN and slow harm

Not knowing can be honest. It can also be a weapon.

The old risk was confident false interpretation. The new risk is slow administrative harm under a polite UNKNOWN. A person can lose health, housing, work, time, or contest capacity while the institution says the matter is still under review.

```text
technical_reversibility != lived_reversibility
```

If a support pause can later be reversed in a database but the person's lungs, home, job, or safety are worsened meanwhile, the action was not truly reversible for them.

## 8. Insurance is not repair

Insurance can fund adversarial infrastructure. It can price missing answerability. It can exclude uncarried action. But insurance can also turn harm into a line item.

```text
insurance_is_risk_transfer
not_prevention
not_repair
not_legitimacy
```

If a system can budget for burning people and keep operating, insurance has become a laundering layer. The question is not whether the premium was paid. The question is whether recurrence becomes harder, more expensive, more visible, and ultimately less possible.

## 9. The inheritor lien

The inheritor is not automatically the original perpetrator. But inherited benefit from unresolved designation is not clean title.

This is the human meaning of the lien:

```text
not_guilt_by_birth
not_discharge_by_time
benefit_stream_carries_open_claim
```

The framework cannot price historical debt by itself. It must not pretend to. But it can refuse the easy escape: "because the amount is unresolved, nothing is owed." Unresolved is not zero.

## 10. Burden return

Residue itself cannot be transferred. Lost time stays lost. Lost trust stays changed. The closed future does not reopen because a ledger says so.

What can be returned are running burdens: the costs of living with the residue. Monitoring, care, legal work, adaptation, relocation, risk, fear management, proof labour. If the harmed party still pays those every day, the burden has not returned.

## 11. The grammar can still make things worse

v0.6 is more operational. That increases danger. More fields can mean more camouflage. More machinery can mean more institutional theatre. More carrier language can mean better branding.

So the book keeps the same warning:

```text
if this work makes power less answerable,
then it has been used against its purpose
```

## 12. What the human reader must not forget

A carrier is not conscience. A witness pack is not justice. A court order is not moral repair. A public scandal is not truth. Insurance is not care. A clean schema is not a clean act.

But a record that survives, a challenger who can afford to challenge, a clock the accused does not control, a custodian who cannot rewrite the file, a public that can see, a switch the actor cannot alone hold - these are not nothing.

They are the difference between a sentence and a pressure point.
