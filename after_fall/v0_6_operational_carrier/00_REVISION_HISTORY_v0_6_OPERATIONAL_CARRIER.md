## Why v0.6 exists

v0.5 answered the v0.4.2 problem that readings without material carrier structures are decorative. It added money, time, custody, succession, and price as carrier masses. External hostile reviews then found that this was not enough. The new failure pattern was not conceptual confusion; it was operational capture.

The convergent defects were:

1. The five masses were incomplete. Reviews repeatedly identified missing force/control, attention/publicity, and technical or operational lockout.
2. The Witness Pack preserved a record but did not yet protect witnesses, resist fake-pack flooding, aggregate safely, or escalate under no-enforcer conditions.
3. ENFORCEMENT_ABSENT could be quoted as a compliance shield unless coupled to no-discharge, witness fallback, and label restrictions.
4. UNKNOWN still permitted slow hardening where an administratively reversible pause created lived irreversibility.
5. Insurance-backed carrier design could price harm as business risk unless prevention and third-party beneficiary rights were made load-bearing.
6. External distribution needed clean current-only readers, a manifest, and a revision history rather than stacked preserved layers.

## v0.6 response

v0.6 is an operational carrier candidate. It does not add a new moral theory. It adds the missing implementation machinery needed to test whether the carrier can survive first contact with retaliation, monopoly power, attention suppression, technical lockout, and label laundering.
