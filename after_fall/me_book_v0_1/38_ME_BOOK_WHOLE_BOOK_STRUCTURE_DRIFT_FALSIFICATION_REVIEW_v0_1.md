## Part structure review

### Part I — Access failure

```trace
Part_I :=
  Chapter_1_false_door
  + Chapter_2_designed_maze
  + Chapter_3_process_distance
```

Function: establishes that procedural existence is not practical access.

Verdict: PASS.

Why: The book begins from a person facing a route rather than from a theory. It establishes the core door claim early and makes the maze/distance sequence feel lived.

Risk: repetition of door/route language is high but earned.

### Part II — Timing failure

```trace
Part_II :=
  Chapter_4_timing_clock
  + Chapter_5_strategic_unknown
  + Chapter_6_slow_harm
```

Function: shows that correction must arrive while it can still help.

Verdict: PASS.

Why: The clock is introduced in human terms before formula. The strategic unknown and slow harm chapters correctly distinguish honest uncertainty from delay that benefits power.

Risk: concept density rises in Chapter 5 and Chapter 6, but not fatally.

### Part III — Witness, custody, residue

```trace
Part_III :=
  Chapter_7_no_enforcer
  + Chapter_8_custody
  + Chapter_9_person_who_still_pays
```

Function: moves from absent enforcement to survival of truth and residue.

Verdict: PASS.

Why: The book does not claim record equals justice. Custody is made practical, and residue lands as the cost still carried after official correction.

Risk: Chapter 8 has a known typo; patch in line pass.

### Part IV — Machine burden

```trace
Part_IV :=
  Chapter_10_maker_complexity
  + Chapter_11_repeat_harm
  + Chapter_12_correction_theatre
```

Function: shows how built systems move burden outward, repeat harm, and then learn the language of correction.

Verdict: PASS_WITH_WATCH.

Why: Part IV is the highest-risk section for systems-theory drift, but it stays book-facing because each chapter returns to person, burden, route, apology, and output.

Risk: Chapter 11 title remains ambiguous. Later title pass should reconsider it.

### Part V — After the fall

```trace
Part_V :=
  Chapter_13_no_fake_reconciliation
  + Chapter_14_what_remains_open
  + Chapter_15_human_standard_for_answerability
```

Function: refuses false moral cleanliness, carries open remainder, and compresses the book into a human standard.

Verdict: PASS.

Why: The register changes correctly after Part IV. The book becomes slower, more final, and less diagnostic. Chapter 15 closes without triumph.

Risk: final chapters use dirty/clean vocabulary heavily. It works now, but line edit should ensure the language remains grounded.
