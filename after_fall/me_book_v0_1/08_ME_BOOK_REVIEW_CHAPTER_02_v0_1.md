## Scope of verdict

This verdict means Chapter 2 has a viable book voice and can stand beside Chapter 1 as part of the ME book spine.

It does not mean the chapter is final, canon, validated, externally ready, or free from later edits.

## Why it passes

### 1. It follows the Chapter 1 pattern without copying it

The chapter opens with a person trying to follow instructions, not with a definition:

```text
The instruction says to follow the process.
```

It widens from the door to the built environment around the door: account creation, email verification, reference numbers, categories, evidence upload, waiting, and escalation.

### 2. The maze emerges step by step

The chapter does not merely assert that systems are mazes. It shows the maze forming from small reasonable requests.

Strong lines:

```text
It sounds simple because each step is simple when it stands alone.

Together, they become a maze.
```

and:

```text
A maze is rarely announced as a maze.

It arrives as small reasonable requests.
```

These are book lines. Keep them.

### 3. It avoids lazy anti-bureaucracy rhetoric

The process caveat works:

```text
Each instruction may have a reason. Identity checks can prevent fraud. Evidence rules can prevent confusion. Deadlines can protect fairness. Portals can create records. Categories can route work. Procedure can stop arbitrary decisions.
```

This prevents the chapter from becoming a generic complaint about forms.

### 4. It names the central distortion clearly

The core sentence is strong:

```text
The problem is that the process can become the institution's version of reality.
```

This is the chapter's real hinge.

### 5. The mismatch sequence works

The sequence beginning with "The route asks..." is precise and memorable:

```text
The route asks for a document. The person has a photograph.
The route asks for a date. The harm happened slowly.
The route asks for one responsible party. The harm came from a chain.
The route asks for a category. The category does not fit.
```

This should remain. It gives shape to the maze without needing technical vocabulary.

### 6. The outside/inside contrast works

The chapter's best paragraph may be:

```text
The account still exists. The reference number still works. The status says pending. The appeal route is still listed. The guidance note is still available. Nothing in the system says the person has been defeated.

But outside the system, the rent is due, the shift starts, the child is ill, the damp spreads, the phone breaks, the message history disappears, the deadline approaches, and the person learns that every attempt to be heard creates another task.
```

This is genuine book prose. It turns formal openness into lived defeat.

### 7. The file/life pattern survives without feeling forced

The chapter reuses the Chapter 1 device:

```text
The file says: route available.

The life says: the route became the burden.
```

This repetition is justified. It may become one of the book's signature rhythms.

### 8. The closing lands

The ending is clean:

```text
The maze is not neutral when the institution built it.

And where an institution designs the maze, it cannot treat exhaustion inside the maze as consent.
```

It closes the chapter without adding notation. Good choice. Chapter 2 should not end with a compact TRACE anchor; Chapter 1 already did that.

## Remaining small issues

These do not block the voice verdict.

### 1. The chapter may be slightly too clean

The prose is controlled and strong, but it may benefit later from one more concrete human object: a screenshot, a bus journey, a locked account, a saved draft, a returned form, or a support worker's note.

Do not patch now unless later chapters feel under-embodied. The chapter currently passes.

### 2. Watch repetition of "route"

The word "route" is necessary, but appears heavily across Chapters 1 and 2. Later chapters should vary the vocabulary with "path," "process," "corridor," "form," "portal," "line," or "way through" where natural.

### 3. The book now needs forward motion

Chapter 1 is the door. Chapter 2 is the maze. Chapter 3 should not simply repeat access failure. It should move to the next idea: process as distance.
