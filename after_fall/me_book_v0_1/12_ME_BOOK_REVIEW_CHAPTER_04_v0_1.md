## Scope of verdict

This means Chapter 4 has a viable book voice and successfully opens the timing/clock section of the ME book.

It does not mean final, canon, validation, proof, release, or external readiness.

## Why it passes

### 1. It changes gear correctly

Chapter 4 does not repeat the access/process arc. It starts with help arriving late:

```text
The apology arrives after the room has been repainted.
```

This is the right opening. It places the reader after correction, where the official system may feel finished but the real loss is not.

### 2. The opening scene works

The first movement is strong:

```text
Something has arrived.

But not everything can still be reached.
```

This is clean book prose. It carries the chapter's distinction without technical language.

### 3. The chapter's load-bearing line works

```text
Correction is not time travel.
```

This should remain. It is memorable, plain, and accurate.

### 4. The clock is introduced at the right time

The formula appears after the human meaning has been established:

```trace
T_det + T_route + T_corr < T_irr
```

This is the correct placement. The chapter earns the notation before using it.

### 5. The formula is bounded properly

The explanation after the formula is important:

```text
That line is not a moral spell. It does not decide the case. It does not tell us what money is owed, who is guilty, or what a court should do.
```

This prevents the book from drifting into fake precision.

### 6. The core clock sentence works

```text
Detection takes time. Routing takes time. Correction takes time. Harm also takes time. The question is which clock wins.
```

This is probably the chapter's central explanatory sentence. Keep it.

### 7. The process caveat remains intact

The chapter avoids claiming that speed is always good:

```text
The point is not that faster is always better.

The point is that delay must be answerable to the harm it allows to harden.
```

This is essential. It keeps the argument serious.

### 8. The ending points naturally to Chapter 5

```text
The next problem is that some systems benefit from not knowing that in time.
```

This is a direct bridge into strategic unknown. Good close.

## Remaining small issues

These do not block the verdict.

### 1. Slight risk of too many aphorisms

The chapter has several strong sentence anchors:

```text
Correction is not time travel.
Help must land before the loss sets.
Eventually is sometimes too late.
The time of help belongs to the harm, not only to the institution.
```

They all work, but later editing should ensure the chapter does not feel over-sloganed. For now, keep them.

### 2. The opening example is composite

The chapter uses a rapid set of examples: room repainted, broken message thread, neighbour moved, child sleep, worker reputation, restored account, corrected debt. This works as a composite pressure field. Later source discipline may require an author's note if the book uses unsourced composite scenes throughout.

### 3. One line may need future tightening

```text
This is the clock underneath the book.
```

This is acceptable, but slightly self-conscious. It can remain for now because the chapter is explicitly introducing the clock layer.
