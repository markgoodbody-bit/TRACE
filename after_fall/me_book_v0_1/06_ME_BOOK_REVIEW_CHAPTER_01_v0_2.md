## Scope of verdict

This verdict means Chapter 1 now has a viable book voice. It does not mean the book is complete, validated, final, or externally ready.

It means the chapter can serve as the voice seed for the ME book.

## Why it passes

### 1. It opens as a book, not a framework

The chapter begins with a scene:

```text
The letter says there is a route.
```

This is the right first move. It gives the reader an object, a situation, and tension before any theory appears.

### 2. The embodied detail fixed the abstraction problem

The added paragraph works:

```text
The printer is in the library. The library closes before the shift ends. The portal times out while the child is crying. The photograph that might help is on a cracked phone, and sending it might reveal the room, the address, or the person who took it.
```

This is the strongest new material. It turns access into lived friction: time, childcare, infrastructure, device fragility, and exposure risk.

### 3. The core sentence remains intact

```text
A door a person cannot afford, understand, reach, or safely open is not a door in their world.
```

This should remain the book's load-bearing sentence.

### 4. The self-naming is now acceptable

```text
Mechanical Ethics begins with that kind of false door.
```

This is better than the previous version. It names the project without sounding like a brand statement.

### 5. The list cluster is now human enough

The revised passage works:

```text
A tenant may have a complaint route and still know that using it could mark them as trouble. A worker may have a safety line and still know who reads the report. A claimant may have an appeal route and still not know what evidence the route is asking for.

The route exists. The risk travels with it.
```

This is book prose. It compresses without becoming sterile.

### 6. The file/life contrast remains the chapter's engine

```text
The file says: no complaint made.
The life says: complaint was too dangerous.
```

This pattern should recur later in the book. It is memorable, plain, and structurally exact.

### 7. The ending now has consequence

```text
The door must be reachable before it shuts. Otherwise the route becomes part of the harm it claims to correct.
```

This lands as a chapter ending and points naturally into the next chapter.

## Remaining small issues

These do not block the voice verdict.

### 1. One repeated self-name remains

The chapter says "Mechanical Ethics" twice. That is acceptable, but the second instance may eventually be removed or converted to "the argument begins there" if the book starts to feel over-named.

### 2. The compact anchor is still slightly spec-like

The `standing := usable_route_not_decorative_access` line works because it appears late. Keep an eye on future chapters. If every chapter ends with notation, the book may drift back into manual voice.

### 3. The examples are still composite

This is fine for now. Later, the book may need a note explaining that early scenes are composite unless specific sourced cases are named.
