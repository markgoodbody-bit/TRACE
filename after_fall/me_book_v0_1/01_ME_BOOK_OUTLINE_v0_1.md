# Mechanical Ethics Book Outline v0.1

Status: working outline. Candidate only. Not canon, validation, proof, permission, clearance, compliance, legal advice, or release.

## Working title

```text
Mechanical Ethics: When Correction Arrives Too Late
```

Alternative titles:

```text
The Door Before It Shuts
A Door You Cannot Reach
The Witness After the File Closes
Correction Is Not Time Travel
```

## One-sentence book promise

This book argues that systems should be judged not by whether they can name a route, issue a correction, or record a loss, but by whether affected people can actually reach answerability before the loss sets.

## Part I — The Door

### Chapter 1 — A Door You Cannot Reach

Core claim:

```text
A right, route, appeal, complaint, review, or remedy that cannot be reached in the person's actual world is not a real door for that person.
```

Human scenes:

```text
a tenant who has a portal but no safe complaint route
a claimant who has an appeal route but no money, time, or understanding
a worker who can report harm only by becoming exposed
```

Structural anchor:

```trace
standing := usable_route_not_decorative_access
```

### Chapter 2 — The Maze Is Not Neutral

Core claim:

```text
Where an institution designs the maze, it cannot treat exhaustion inside the maze as consent.
```

Topics:

```text
complexity
forms
deadlines

professional language
proof burden
```

### Chapter 3 — Process Becomes Distance

Core claim:

```text
Procedure can protect fairness, but it can also move the harmed person farther from the thing that would repair the harm.
```

Topics:

```text
administrative delay
referrals
escalation ladders
case closure
non-response
```

## Part II — The Clock

### Chapter 4 — Help Before the Loss Sets

Core claim:

```text
Correction that arrives after irreversibility is not full repair.
```

### Chapter 15 — A Human Standard for Answerability

Core claim:

```text
Answerability is not a tone of remorse. It is a route by which affected consequence can change future action.
```

Closing anchor:

```text
The door must be reachable before it shuts. Help must land before the loss sets. The record must outlive the power that tried to exhaust it.
```
