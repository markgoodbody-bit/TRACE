## Scope of verdict

This means Chapter 10 has a viable book voice and successfully opens Part IV by turning from witness/residue to the machinery that keeps producing burden.

It does not mean final, canon, validation, proof, release, or external readiness.

## Why it passes

### 1. It starts from the person's contact with complexity

The chapter opens correctly:

```text
The person is told the system is complicated.

They already know.
```

This is a strong first turn. It refuses the institutional excuse before naming the theory.

### 2. Complexity is made visible as design, not weather

The chapter's strongest early distinction is:

```text
The mistake is treating it like weather.
```

The rain comparison works because it separates unavoidable natural difficulty from built institutional complexity.

### 3. It avoids pretending complexity is always avoidable

The chapter keeps the required caveat:

```text
Some complexity is unavoidable.
```

It also says simple systems can be cruel. This protects the argument from becoming naive simplicity worship.

### 4. The maker-cost argument lands

```text
Complexity is not weightless.

Someone pays for it.
```

This is the chapter's core compression. Keep it.

### 5. The fragment/aggregate distinction is strong

```text
A system cannot defend itself in fragments while harming in aggregate.
```

This is a load-bearing Part IV sentence. It should remain.

### 6. Responsibility is separated from blame

The chapter correctly says:

```text
Responsibility is not the same as blame.
```

and:

```text
But absence of blame in each part cannot become absence of responsibility in the whole.
```

This keeps the machine critique serious. It avoids turning frontline staff into scapegoats while still preserving whole-system answerability.

### 7. The unpaid systems analyst line works

```text
A serious system should not wait for the harmed person to become an unpaid systems analyst.
```

This is a strong human-facing line. Keep it.

### 8. It closes correctly toward repeat harm

```text
The next problem is repetition.

Because a machine that charges its complexity outward once will usually do it again, unless something in the machine is forced to learn.
```

This is a clean bridge into Chapter 11.

## Remaining small issues

These do not block the verdict.

### 1. The chapter is structurally strong but slightly abstract in the middle

The passage on maker responsibility is clear, but later edit may add one embodied detail around the person dealing with the portal/call centre chain: a phone battery dying, a lunch break ending, a reference number copied onto paper, or a child waiting. Do not patch now.

### 2. Part IV needs examples of repeat harm

Chapter 11 should use a concrete repeated-failure pattern, not just say systems repeat errors. It should show one person becoming many people.

### 3. Avoid turning Part IV into a systems-theory lecture

Chapter 10 stays book-like. Chapter 11 must keep that discipline under pressure.
