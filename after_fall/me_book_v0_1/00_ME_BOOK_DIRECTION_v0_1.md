# Mechanical Ethics Book Direction v0.1

Status: working direction for the human-facing ME book. Candidate only. Not canon, validation, proof, permission, clearance, compliance, legal advice, or release.

## 1. Decision

The ME human layer should become an actual human book, not only a booklet or technical appendix.

```trace
ME_book_project := active_human_artifact
```

The book is one of the two real After-Fall artifacts:

```trace
After_Fall := TRACE_packet + ME_book
```

TRACE carries the grammar and operational machinery. ME carries the human meaning and moral argument.

## 2. What the book is for

The book should make a reader understand why the machinery matters before they see the machinery.

It should begin from human situations:

```text
a complaint route that exists but cannot be used
a correction that arrives after the loss has set
a system that benefits from complexity
a witness whose record has no place to stand
a residue that remains after the file is closed
```

The book's task is not to prove TRACE. It is to make the structural truth legible in human terms.

## 3. Core thesis

```text
A door a person cannot afford, understand, reach, or safely open is not a door in their world.
```

Everything else grows from this.

## 4. Book role

```trace
ME_book_role :=
  human_front_door
  + moral_argument
  + lived_examples
  + anti_laundering_language
  + pressure_against_fake_correction
```

## 5. What the book must not become

```trace
not := technical_spec
not := legal_treatise
not := governance_checklist
not := outreach_pitch
not := validation_claim
not := motivational_manifesto
not := AI_policy_comparison
```

The book can use examples from law, public administration, technology, housing, work, care, and ordinary life. It should not collapse into policy procedure or framework promotion.

## 6. Reader

The primary reader is not a mathematician or systems engineer.

The reader may be:

```text
someone harmed by a process
someone who works inside a process
someone who has watched an institution correct too late
someone who senses that official routes can be false doors
someone building or challenging systems
someone trying to understand why a record matters even when no one listens yet
```

## 7. Style target

Plain, serious, spare, concrete.

The writing should use short scenes, clear claims, and repeated human anchors.

Glyphs and formulas may appear as compact anchors, but only after the human meaning is already visible.

```trace
glyphs := occasional_structural_anchors
not := main_texture
```

## 8. Relationship to TRACE and Witness Pack

The book should not explain every carrier field.

It should make the reader understand why these things matter:

```text
usable route
late correction
strategic unknown
custody
witness
residue
repeat harm
burden return
no fake reconciliation
```

TRACE and the Witness Pack remain the operational packet. The book is the human argument that gives them sense.

## 9. First build objective

Create the book spine, style rules, chapter plan, and one real sample chapter.

Do not try to finish the whole book in one pass.

```trace
first_build :=
  direction
  + outline
  + style_rules
  + sample_chapter
```

End.
