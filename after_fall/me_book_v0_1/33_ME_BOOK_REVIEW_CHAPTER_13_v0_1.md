## Scope of verdict

This means Chapter 13 has a viable book voice and successfully changes the register for Part V. It slows down after the machine-facing Part IV and turns toward residue, false closure, and moral cleanliness.

It does not mean final, canon, validation, proof, release, or external readiness.

## Why it passes

### 1. It opens at the right moment

```text
The apology has been given.
```

This is the correct post-correction opening. The chapter begins after the official action, where pressure for closure starts.

### 2. It makes closure pressure human, not cartoonish

```text
People begin to look for closure.
```

The chapter does not make everyone cruel. It shows relief, fatigue, hope, and institutional desire to move on. That is more truthful than making closure pressure a simple villain.

### 3. The core claim lands

```text
Some losses can be recorded, carried, and answered for, but not made clean.
```

This is the right Part V sentence. It is slower, more final, and less diagnostic than Part IV.

### 4. It refuses comfort on both sides

The chapter says the sentence refuses both the system's comfort and the fantasy that every wrong can be balanced if the account is detailed enough. This protects the chapter from becoming either institutional absolution or total compensation calculus.

### 5. The embodied loss paragraph works

```text
The missed chance remains missed. The fear remains learned. The body remembers the damp room, the long wait, the public accusation, the unsafe route, the months spent proving what should have been checked earlier.
```

This restores lived consequence without melodrama.

### 6. The consent/choice boundary is correct

```text
A person may choose, freely and for their own reasons, to forgive, settle, stop fighting, stop speaking, accept an apology, or build a life around what cannot be restored.

That choice belongs to them.

It cannot be demanded as proof that correction worked.
```

This is essential. It refuses fake reconciliation without denying the person's own agency to forgive or stop.

### 7. The extraction sequence works

```text
The system took time.

Then it took labour.

Then it took evidence.

Then it took patience.

Now it wants peace.
```

This is one of the strongest passages. Keep it.

### 8. The central clarification is strong

```text
Refusing fake reconciliation is not refusing peace.

It is refusing to call a dirty ending clean.
```

This should remain.

### 9. The institutional-wisdom passage works

```text
The first person is not raw material for institutional wisdom.
```

This is a strong moral line and fits the book's anti-laundering spine.

### 10. The ending bridges correctly to Chapter 14

```text
The next question is what remains open when everyone else wants the matter closed.
```

This is an effective Chapter 14 handoff.

## Remaining small issues

These do not block the verdict.

### 1. The word "cleaning" is now doing heavy work

The chapter uses cleaning as a recurring anti-laundering term. It works, but a later line edit should check whether the word is too compressed for cold readers without the full previous chapters.

### 2. Slight risk of over-finality

The chapter is meant to be slow and final, but Chapter 14 still needs energy. Chapter 14 should not merely repeat that something remains open. It should specify how open debt, memory, future obligation, and custody across time are held.

### 3. No new system categories should be added in Part V

This chapter obeys that. Chapter 14 should also obey it.
