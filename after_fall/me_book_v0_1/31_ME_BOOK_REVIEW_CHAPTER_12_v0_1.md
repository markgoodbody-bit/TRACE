## Scope of verdict

This means Chapter 12 has a viable book voice and successfully closes Part IV by distinguishing performed correction language from correction that moves burden, route, authority, or mechanism.

It does not mean final, canon, validation, proof, release, or external readiness.

## Why it passes

### 1. It opens from performed seriousness, not theory

```text
The statement sounds serious.
```

This is the right entry. The chapter begins with a recognisable formal correction statement rather than a definition.

### 2. It places the harmed person beside the statement

```text
The person reads it twice.
```

This keeps the chapter human-facing. The person is not merely the subject of correction language; they are the one who has to live after it.

### 3. The core contrast works

```text
But the person still has to use the same route tomorrow.
```

This is the chapter's first hard test. Correction language is judged against what the person must still use.

### 4. The main definition is clear

```text
Theatre begins when correction language performs seriousness while leaving the burden-producing mechanism intact.
```

This is strong and should remain.

### 5. It distinguishes theatre from incomplete sincerity

The chapter correctly refuses the lazy move of treating every imperfect correction as theatre:

```text
A fake correction and an incomplete correction can look similar from outside.
```

and:

```text
Mechanical Ethics does not treat every imperfect correction as theatre.
```

This is essential. It keeps the argument serious and avoids bad-faith flattening.

### 6. The moved-burden test works

The chapter asks the right question:

```text
It asks what moved.
```

The follow-up questions are strong because they test usability, timing, evidence burden, authority, residue, challengeability, and mechanism change.

### 7. The hidden-transfer section is one of the strongest passages

```text
The system keeps the authority to define what counts as improvement.

The person keeps the cost of finding out whether anything changed.
```

This is the chapter's most precise anti-laundering point. Keep it.

### 8. The auditor line lands

```text
The correction asks the harmed person to become the auditor of the correction.
```

This should remain. It names burden return after correction performance.

### 9. It closes Part IV cleanly

```text
A system can become fluent in the language of repair while remaining structurally bad at being repaired by those it harms.
```

This is an excellent Part IV closing sentence. The final bridge to Part V works:

```text
The next part has to refuse false cleanliness.
```

## Remaining small issues

These do not block the verdict.

### 1. The chapter is conceptually dense but stable

It has many diagnostic sentences and question sequences. They work because Chapter 12 is closing Part IV, but a later book edit may trim one question cluster.

### 2. Watch repeated “language” phrasing

The repeated pattern:

```text
Correction is not the same as correction language.
Learning is not the same as learning language.
```

works as a closing drumbeat, but it should not be repeated in later chapters.

### 3. Part V must change the register

Part IV has been machine-facing. Part V should become starker, slower, and more final. It should not continue adding more diagnostic categories. It should face irreparable residue and false reconciliation.
