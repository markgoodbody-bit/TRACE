## Scope of verdict

This means Chapter 5 has a viable book voice and successfully develops the strategic-unknown layer after Chapter 4's timing chapter.

It does not mean final, canon, validation, proof, release, or external readiness.

## Why it passes

### 1. It starts from fair uncertainty

The chapter opens correctly:

```text
The institution says it does not yet know.

That may be true.
```

This is crucial. The book does not treat all uncertainty as bad faith. It first makes room for honest investigation.

### 2. The honest-uncertainty caveat works

The passage about the danger of knowing too quickly is strong:

```text
A system that pretends to know too quickly can do real harm.
```

This gives the chapter credibility and keeps it from collapsing into suspicion.

### 3. The hinge is clear and memorable

```text
The honest unknower carries part of the burden of not knowing.

The strategic unknower spends the unknown itself.
```

This is the chapter's central hinge. Keep it.

### 4. The file/life pattern still works

The file/life contrast is precise here:

```text
The file says: insufficient information.

The life says: the information is in your system.
```

The pattern remains useful because it now targets uncertainty, not merely process or access.

### 5. It correctly avoids a villain requirement

The chapter says:

```text
Sometimes it is deliberate. Often it is not. A system does not need a villain for useful ignorance to appear.
```

This is important. The book is stronger when it shows structural benefit without requiring cartoon malice.

### 6. The burden question is strong

```text
The question is not only what is unknown.

The question is who pays while it remains unknown.
```

This should remain one of the book's key sentences.

### 7. Honest investigation versus useful suspension is clear

The contrast between honest investigation and useful suspension is one of the best practical sections in the chapter.

```text
Honest investigation says: we do not know yet, so here is what we will do to find out...

Useful suspension says: we do not know yet, so nothing changes for you.
```

This makes the moral distinction usable without needing formal machinery.

### 8. The ending points correctly to slow harm

The final sentence:

```text
The next problem is slow harm: the kind of loss that hardens while everyone still says the matter is open.
```

This is a clean bridge to Chapter 6.

## Remaining small issues

These do not block the verdict.

### 1. The chapter is conceptually dense

It passes, but it is denser than Chapters 1-4. Later revision may want one more concrete object near the middle: a locked file, missing call recording, incomplete contractor note, unproduced log, or unanswered evidence request.

### 2. Watch aphorism accumulation

The chapter contains several strong lines. They work individually, but the book should avoid sounding like a chain of maxims. Later pass should vary rhythm with more scene where needed.

### 3. Repetition of "unknown" is high but justified

The word is central to the chapter. Do not patch now. Later line editing can reduce repetitions where the argument remains clear.
