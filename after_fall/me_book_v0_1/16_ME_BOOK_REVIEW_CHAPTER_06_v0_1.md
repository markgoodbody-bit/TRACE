## Scope of verdict

This means Chapter 6 has a viable book voice and successfully closes Part II by turning the timing/unknown argument into slow hardening.

It does not mean final, canon, validation, proof, release, or external readiness.

## Why it passes

### 1. It opens with the right contradiction

```text
The case is still open.
```

This is the correct opening for slow harm. It starts with technical openness and lets the chapter show why openness is not the same as safety.

### 2. The slow-hardening sequence works

```text
The first week is inconvenience. The second is worry. The third is borrowing. The fourth is silence becoming normal. The fifth is a missed appointment because the person no longer trusts that anything will change. The sixth is a new problem caused by the old one.
```

This is the strongest early passage. It shows accumulation without melodrama.

### 3. It makes waiting visible

The paragraph beginning "It looks like checking the portal before work" works because it shows slow harm as ordinary behaviour rather than a dramatic event.

This keeps the chapter human and concrete.

### 4. Technical reversibility versus lived reversibility is clear

The reversible-list passage works:

```text
A deduction can be reversed.
A record can be corrected.
An account can be reopened.
A form can be resubmitted.
A complaint can be accepted late.
A wall can be repainted.
```

The response is also strong:

```text
On paper, the system can still undo the entry.

In life, the entry has already travelled.
```

This is the chapter's central distinction.

### 5. The file/life pattern still carries meaning

```text
The file says: no final decision yet.

The life says: the lack of a decision is making decisions for me.
```

This is one of the best uses of the pattern so far. It names slow harm exactly.

### 6. The undercounting passage works

The repeated "They count..." sequence is effective because it shows how institutions can count the official object while missing the consequential spread.

Keep the sequence.

### 7. The chapter lands the Part II close

```text
A record is not justice.

But without a record, slow harm has an easy way to disappear.
```

This closes Part II and opens Part III cleanly.

## Remaining small issues

These do not block the verdict.

### 1. The chapter is long but justified

It carries several conceptual jobs: slow hardening, lived reversibility, absorption, undercounting, and bridge to witness/record. It holds together, but later book-level edit may trim repetition.

### 2. The file/life pattern is nearing saturation

It still works here, but Part III should use it more sparingly or vary the rhythm. Otherwise the book may begin to feel mechanically patterned.

### 3. Part III must change the energy

The next section should not simply continue the same harm-analysis mode. It needs to introduce witness, custody, record, and absence of enforcer. The prose should become slightly more severe and less diagnostic.
