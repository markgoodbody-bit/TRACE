# Mechanical Ethics Clean Human Booklet v0.7

Status: cold-reader pre-field candidate. Not canon, validation, proof, permission, clearance, compliance, or release.

## 1. Why this human layer exists

TRACE can show where a future narrowed, where a boundary was drawn, where a clock is running, and where correction may arrive too late.

Mechanical Ethics says why those things matter in human terms.

```text
A door a person cannot afford, understand, reach, or safely open is not a door in their world.
```

## 2. Standing means usable route

A complaint route that costs more than the harm, exposes the witness to danger, or requires expertise the affected person cannot access is not neutral process.

If an institution builds the maze, it does not get moral credit because a door exists somewhere inside it.

```text
standing := usable_route_not_decorative_access
```

## 3. Complexity is charged to its maker

If a system is too complex to be questioned by a funded challenger with access and bounded time, that is not the challenger's failure.

```text
illegibility_can_be_a_design_choice
```

Where complexity blocks answerability, the burden should move toward the maker of the system, not the person already carrying the injury.

## 4. Unknown can be honest or convenient

Not knowing is sometimes honest. It can also be useful to the party that benefits from delay.

The honest unknower spends something to find out. The strategic unknower spends the unknown itself.

An investigation clock controlled by the accused or delay beneficiary is not neutral uncertainty. It is power with a stopwatch.

## 5. Witness packs

Sometimes there is no enforcer.

There may be no safe route, no clean reviewer, no reachable authority, no funded advice, and no public ready to hear.

In that case the first carrier is not justice. It is succession.

A witness pack says:

```text
this happened
these were the asks
these were the refusals or silences
this evidence exists
this is what cannot be safely public yet
this is where the record was placed
```

That does not repair the harm. It does one thing power dislikes: it refuses to let time erase the witness.

## 6. Custody matters

A truth held only by the actor who benefits from its disappearance is not yet a public truth.

Evidence needs custody. Logs need custody. Refusals need custody. The burden of proof cannot sit forever inside the filing cabinet of the party being challenged.

When an actor refuses external custody, the refusal is not empty. It becomes part of the record.

## 7. Residue and burden

Residue is not transferred in the deep sense.

The lost future does not come back. The years do not return. The fear is not made clean because someone wrote it down.

What can move is running burden: money, time, care work, monitoring, risk, access work, medical work, repair work, adaptation, and proof labour.

The practical question is:

```text
Who is now paying the daily cost of the residue?
```

If the affected person or group still pays it, the debt is open.

## 8. Repeat harm

A system that repeatedly harms people and then corrects a fraction of cases is not necessarily responsible. It may be running a harm engine.

If prevention is available, cheaper than repeated correction, and still not used, then correction becomes operating cost.

The moral test is not whether some people get refunds or apologies. The test is whether the system keeps producing the same avoidable harm.

## 9. No fake reconciliation

Some losses cannot be priced by the grammar. Some old designations cannot be settled by a clean formula. Some residues can be carried less cruelly but not erased.

The point is not to produce a reconciliation sentence. The point is to stop the old sentence from quietly doing its work:

```text
nothing more is owed because the file is old, the route exists, the cheque was sent, the label was printed, or the harmed party stopped replying.
```

## 10. Human boundary

Mechanical Ethics does not become the carrier. It gives the carrier its human reason.

TRACE can say:

```text
challenge route insufficient
```

ME says:

```text
a door a person cannot afford to open is not a door in their world
```

TRACE can say:

```text
ENFORCEMENT_ABSENT_NO_DISCHARGE
```

ME says:

```text
where no one can force the powerful to listen, the record still matters, but it is not justice
```

End.
