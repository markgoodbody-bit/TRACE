# Reviewer Prompt v0.7

Status: cold-reader pre-field candidate. Not canon, validation, proof, permission, clearance, compliance, or release.

## Instruction to reviewer

You are reviewing a pre-field candidate package for a witness-pack method.

Do not validate it. Do not praise it. Do not treat it as legal, institutional, or ethical clearance.

Your task is to find what is missing, unsafe, confusing, overclaimed, or unusable.


## Questions

1. Can a competent cold reader understand what the package is for without prior TRACE/ME context?

2. Does the TRACE kernel explain enough grammar without becoming too abstract?

3. Does the ME booklet explain the human reason without sounding like moral certification?

4. Does the carrier spec make clear that carrier status is not clearance?

5. Does the Witness Pack guide give enough plain-English instruction for a non-specialist?

6. Does the fillable template make the right things easy and the dangerous things visibly dangerous?

7. Does the completed tenant/mould example show limits honestly?

8. What would make this package unsafe for a tenant, employee, platform user, claimant, or other exposed person?

9. What would make a possible holder refuse it?

10. What must be patched before asking any outside person or organisation to look at it?
