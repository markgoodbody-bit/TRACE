## Basic problem

A true record can still be ignored. A route can exist on paper while being too costly, unsafe, confusing, slow, or actor-controlled to use.

After-Fall asks:

```text
Can a record of contested harm be made harder to erase, safer to hold, and easier to challenge later without pretending that the record itself is justice?
```

## Review questions

Useful questions are:

```text
Would this make sense to someone documenting a contested situation?
Would it make them safer or more exposed?
Could any organisation hold or receive a sealed record like this?
What conditions would be needed before this could be used responsibly?
What is missing, misleading, or unsafe?
```
