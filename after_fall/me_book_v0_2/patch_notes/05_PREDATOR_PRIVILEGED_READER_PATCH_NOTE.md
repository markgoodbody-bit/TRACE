# ME Book v0.2 Patch Note — Predator and Privileged Reader

Status: patch note for v0.2.1 scope. Candidate only. Not canon, validation, proof, permission, clearance, compliance, legal advice, publication readiness, or release.

Trigger:

```text
Mark: yes it needs more understanding of the predator and the privileged
```

## Core correction

Fable's council named the missing pressure as reader implication. Mark's addition sharpens it:

```text
The book must understand not only the harmed person and the witness, but also the predator, the beneficiary, and the privileged reader.
```

If the book only speaks to harmed people and decent witnesses, it becomes consolation. After-Fall needs more than consolation.

It must also make visible the person who benefits from the door being unreachable.

## Boundary

This must not become cartoon villain theory.

```text
predator != monster_only
privileged != evil_by_default
beneficiary != conscious_designer_always
```

The pressure is subtler and stronger:

```text
some people hunt the weak spots
some people inherit the weak spots
some people rent comfort from the weak spots
some people administer the weak spots
some people can ignore the weak spots because the door opens for them
```

## Predator understanding

The predator is not only the person who breaks a rule.

The predator may be the person who understands the delay better than the victim does.

The predator knows:

```text
the complaint route is slow
the tenant is tired
the worker is frightened
the platform appeal will not answer in time
the evidence burden is too high
the person cannot afford the next step
the regulator rarely bites
the file will call silence consent
```

Predation often works by using clean systems.

It does not always need illegal force. It can use delay, category, cost, fear, opacity, reputation, exhaustion, and the formal availability of routes that will not arrive in time.

Candidate human line:

```text
A predator does not need the door to be locked if they know the person cannot reach it before the harm is done.
```

## Privileged reader understanding

The privileged reader is not simply rich, cruel, or knowingly guilty.

The privileged reader may be ordinary, kind, careful, and still protected by arrangements that make other people absorb the cost.

Privilege here means:

```text
my complaint is believed sooner
my route is explained better
my delay is treated as reasonable
my missing document is chased, not punished
my anger is called frustration, not difficulty
my risk is seen as urgent
my silence is not used against me
my door opens because someone expects me to belong there
```

The book must make this chair visible.

Candidate human line:

```text
Sometimes the reader is not the person at the door. Sometimes the reader is the person whose door opens because someone else's did not.
```

## Designation power

The missing formal piece is D/designation in human language:

```text
who counts
whose loss counts
which boundary counts
which category a person is placed inside
which silence is treated as consent
which anger is treated as risk
which evidence is treated as evidence
```

This must appear without jargon.

Candidate human line:

```text
The deepest power is not only deciding what will be done. It is deciding whose loss counts as loss before anything is done at all.
```

## Tone rule

The book should not flatter the harmed reader by making them morally pure, and should not let the privileged reader remain untouched.

It should say, in effect:

```text
You may be harmed in one system and privileged in another.
You may be witness in one case and beneficiary in another.
You may hate cruelty and still benefit from a route being unusable to someone else.
You may not have built the old designation, but if you inherit its power you inherit the duty not to hide behind it.
```
