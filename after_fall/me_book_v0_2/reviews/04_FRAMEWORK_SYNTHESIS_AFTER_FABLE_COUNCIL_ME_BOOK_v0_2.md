## Accepted defects before cold reader

The following defects should be patched before a first external human cold read:

### Defect 1 — Reader never becomes debtor

Current risk:

```text
reader_can_stay_harmed_person_or_witness_only
comfortable_side_not_implicated
```

Required recovery:

```text
reader_as_beneficiary_of_old_designations
reader_as_landlord_manager_designer_reviewer_comfortable_side
who_counts_is_power
D/designation_without_jargon
```

Patch form:

```text
Add narrow implicating passage, probably in Chapter 14 or Chapter 15.
Do not turn it into accusation theatre.
Do not accuse every reader equally.
Make the comfortable chair visible.
```

Candidate human line:

```text
Sometimes the reader is not the person at the door. Sometimes the reader is the person whose door opens because someone else's did not.
```

### Defect 2 — Machine-speed stakes missing

Current risk:

```text
Part_IV_machine := bureaucracy_only
After_Fall_stakes_hidden
```

Required recovery:

```text
same false doors + same clocks + same disappearing records + machine speed
no AI-policy chapter
no techno-panic
bridge only
```

Patch form:

```text
Add short bridge at opening of Part IV or Chapter 10.
Say that the old machinery is now being accelerated and automated.
The danger is not that machines are strange; it is that familiar failures can now run faster than human correction.
```

Candidate human line:

```text
The fall is not only that machines may decide. It is that the old unreachable door can now close faster than a person can even find the handle.
```

### Defect 3 — No complete case / no honest defeat

Current risk:

```text
fragments_are_strong_but_no_case_thread
framework_never_loses_on_page
```

Required recovery:

```text
one composite case followed from door to residue
one unrescued failure
no miracle correction
no validation theatre
```

Patch form:

```text
Either thread a single composite case lightly across existing chapters, or add one short interlude/case section without changing the 15-chapter spine.
Do not create a manual.
Do not create a legal case study.
The point is narrative traction and honest loss.
```

Candidate human line:

```text
No one came in time. The record survived. The wall did not become safe soon enough. The debt did not disappear. That is not a success story. It is the kind of story the system prefers not to keep whole.
```
