## A. One-line verdict under claim ceiling

v0.2 is the right human-facing direction — the voice is finally the book's own and the spine holds — but it is currently a gentler book than the project needs: readable in the sentence, unproven in the average reader, and missing the two moves that would make it dangerous rather than sad.

## B. Three strongest survivals

1. **The file-says/life-says double ledger.** This is the book's engine, not a decoration: it teaches the entire grammar — two accounts of one event, the system's and the person's — by rhythm alone, with zero concepts required. It is the strongest single accessibility device in anything this project has produced.

2. **Chapter 5, The Strategic Unknown.** The most complete chapter in the book: "uncertainty has a direction," the spend test, who-pays-while-it-stays-unknown — an entire formal apparatus rendered fully human with nothing lost.

3. **The unclean-endings discipline (Ch13–14).** "Some losses can be recorded, carried, and answered for, but not made clean." "Open does not mean infinite. Open means not falsely closed." "It has outsourced memory to pain." The anti-theatre pressure survived compression intact, and this is where the witness register is purest.

## C. Five defects, ranked

1. **The reader is never the debtor.** Every reader gets to be the harmed person or witness; nobody reading this book is ever the landlord. A book about answerability that never implicates its reader is a consolation. The book also lacks the plain statement that deciding whose loss counts is itself the deepest exercise of power.

2. **The fall has no machines in it.** Part IV's "Machine" is entirely bureaucratic. The book does not say that this same machinery is now being built to run at machine speed, with the same unreachable doors, clocks, and vanishing records. Without that bridge, the After-Fall human book does not know which fall it is after.

3. **No complete case; no un-rescued failure.** The composite scenes are excellent fragments, but no single story is followed from door to residue, and the framework never loses on-page.

4. **Chapter 15 ends as litany, not landing.** The chapter's true spine is the definition of answerability, but it sits inside stacked list-runs. The composite person has left the room before the end.

5. **Part IV addressee drift, and the Ch11 title.** Parts I–III speak from beside the person; Part IV increasingly speaks across the desk to management. "Repeat Harm Is Not Responsibility" parses wrong on first read. The chapter's true title may be "The Second Person."
