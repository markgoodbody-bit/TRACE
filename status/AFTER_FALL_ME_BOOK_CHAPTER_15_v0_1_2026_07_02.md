## Chapter claim

```text
Answerability is not a tone of remorse. It is a route by which affected consequence can change future action.
```

## Drafting basis

Followed Chapter 14 voice review constraints:

```text
start with a person facing a route, a clock, and a record
compress the whole book rather than adding new machinery
return to the three closing anchors: reachable door, timely help, surviving record
make answerability human and practical, not abstract virtue language
avoid sounding like a manifesto or framework pitch
include TRACE/ME only lightly, if at all
close the book with a sober standard, not triumph
```
