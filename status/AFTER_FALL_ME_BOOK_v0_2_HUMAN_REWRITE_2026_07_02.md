## Why this exists

Mark read the v0.1 PDF and found that it basically made sense, but was too dense and not yet average-human-facing enough.

The v0.2 rewrite keeps the same After-Fall ME book spine but lowers cognitive load.

## Style target

Original voice, not imitation.

Working blend:

```text
plain public clarity
+ humane systems imagination
+ witness-pressure
```

The aim is:

```text
recognisable to an ordinary thoughtful human
serious without being academic
moral without sermon
clear without flattening
less dense than v0.1
```

## What changed from v0.1

```text
same 15-chapter structure

lighter appendix
```
